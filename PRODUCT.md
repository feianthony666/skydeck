# SkyDeck

SkyDeck is a live aviation intelligence dashboard for pilots, aviation enthusiasts, and operations teams. It presents ADS-B traffic, airport information, weather, datalink activity, and flight safety context in a dense, fast, familiar operations interface.

## Users

- Pilots planning or monitoring a flight
- Dispatchers and aviation operations staff
- Enthusiasts tracking live and historical aircraft activity

## Product purpose

Make current flight and airport information understandable at a glance while preserving direct access to source and detail views.
